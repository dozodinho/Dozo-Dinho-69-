ABOUT DOZO DINHO 69

My story is not about where I started. It is about where I decided to go.

I came from a humble background, and life taught me early that success does not always begin with comfort. I experienced seasons of struggle, financial pressure and difficult decisions. See through the struggle, there is always a way forward if you are willing to work and learn.

In the early 2020s, I discovered trading and began studying the markets. By 2023 and 2024, I was actively trading and learning through real experience. There were wins, losses, mistakes and lessons. Not every day in the market taught me to be rich. Most days taught me to be humble, patient and disciplined.

Those experiences shaped the purpose behind this platform. I do not present myself as someone who has never failed. I want to share what failure, discipline and persistence taught me — so others can learn faster, avoid preventable mistakes, and build a stronger foundation in trading and in life.

LESSONS FROM THE JOURNEY

What I learned, I want others to learn earlier.

01

Do not chase quick money.

Desperation can make bad opportunities look attractive. Learn first and make decisions with patience.

02

Be careful who you trust online.

Scams and false promises are real. Verify people, services and opportunities before sending money.

03

Do not trade money you cannot afford to lose.

Debt and emotional pressure can make trading decisions even harder. Protect your foundation.

04

Failure can become education.

Mistakes are painful, but they can become valuable lessons when you are willing to reflect and improve.
